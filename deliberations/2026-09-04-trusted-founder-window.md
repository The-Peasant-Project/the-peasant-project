# Question

In stage one and early stage two of the pathway, the person running the software is trusted. The size-limit ratchet shortens this. Can the ledger be tamper-evident from day one, and should a second member joining automatically require third-party-verifiable records?

# Strongest positions

Close it immediately: an append-only, hash-chained ledger costs almost nothing and removes the trust question before it starts.

Leave it open: friction at the moment a second person joins kills adoption; trust between two neighbours is fine and the ratchet at ten is enough.
