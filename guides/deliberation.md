# How we deliberate

For any question that could change a rule, a design constant, or the philosophy. Not for typos or wording.

One file per question in `deliberations/`, named `YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug.md`, using the template in that folder. The file holds the question, the strongest positions, responses gathered from several models and from people, an honest synthesis, and either a decision log entry or a clear statement of why it stays open.

Rules. At least two different model families and at least one human respond before synthesis. Each response is labelled with who or what produced it. The synthesis names where the responses disagree; it does not paper over. The oracle may write the synthesis and propose the decision. A human signs the decision. Nothing in `docs/` or `AGENTS.md` changes until the decision log has the entry.

Why so heavy: rules that can be changed by whoever is holding the keyboard, human or model, are how a project becomes a fiefdom.
