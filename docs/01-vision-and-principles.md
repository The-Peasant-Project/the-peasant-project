# Vision and principles

## From and toward

We are moving away from a way of seeing the world as something to extract from and compete over. We are moving toward seeing it as something to care for, partner with and take part in. That is not a mood. It is a set of rules about who owns what, where money goes, and who decides.

## The ten principles

1. Prosperity is shared per person, not per dollar invested.
2. Providing hardware earns cost plus a capped return, never control.
3. Wealth that sits still must move.
4. Cooperation is rewarded by the system, not by asking nicely.
5. Cheating is visible and its cost is automatic.
6. Membership carries obligations as well as rights.
7. Decisions are made by members; experts publish options with costs.
8. A slice of everything belongs to people not yet born.
9. Anyone can leave, and take their node, their data and the protocol with them.
10. Everything is open, so nothing can be fenced.

## What prosperity means here

Not accumulation. Prosperity is a warm pool in winter, a cheque that arrives without conditions, a town that can fix its own machines, and the knowledge that the next town over is building because you helped. It is measured in what flows, not what piles up.
