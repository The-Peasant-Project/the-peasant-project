# Biology: enforced reciprocity and partner choice

Intended content: mycorrhizal nutrient trading as partner choice; why the popular story of generous trees is overstated; multicellular bodies, surveillance and self-removal of cells that stop reciprocating; slime moulds and ant colonies as examples of coordination without a boss; what each maps to in Understory (reciprocity score, routing weight, sweep, ledger transparency); what biology warns against (parasites that mimic cooperators, arms races).
