# Learning resources

One idea per resource, built to `guides/explainer.md`. These are parallel to the playbook, not a summary of it: the playbook says what Understory is; these teach the ideas that make it make sense, to someone who has never heard of it.

Planned, in the order a newcomer should meet them:

1. Why your app store takes a third. Techno-feudalism from a single receipt.
2. Why the sun gets switched off. Curtailment from a single sunny afternoon.
3. Why the fungus stops feeding the stingy tree. Enforced reciprocity from one experiment.
4. Why a jury beats an election for a small board. Sortition from one committee meeting.
5. Why money that sits still should move. The sweep from one club's bank balance.
6. Why a fork button is a right, not a feature. Exit from one migration story.

Each one: a markdown page, one diagram, under a thousand words, ends with something the reader does. Each is also the seed of a short video.
